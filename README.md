<h2>Overview</h2>

The inspiration for it came from my gym in real life and the -- in my opinion -- outdated logging system. The old method got the job done but it was a pain to enter the data and keep track of it. 

I created this site as a way to show how it could be improved through a simple, more convenient web interface.

<h2> Design Philosophy</h2>

I went for a minimalistic approach as the intended users are gym members who want to quickly record their information and get on with their workouts. 

Rather than adding unnecesary visual elements or feedback, the interface prioritizes simplicity and ease of use. 

<h2>How it works</h2>

1. Enter the required information into the form
2. Hit the **Log** button to save the entry locally in your browser
3. View your record in the table
